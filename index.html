RefinedOps Publisher

A private publishing tool for The Refined G and Gaijin GT-R

RefinedOps Publisher is a personal content-publishing tool operated by Justin Eplin. It lets him publish videos he created himself to his own social media accounts, including the TikTok accounts @therefinedg (The Refined G) and @gaijingtr (Gaijin GT-R), from his own computer.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the account owner signs in, and only his own accounts are connected.
