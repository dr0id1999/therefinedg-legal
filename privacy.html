Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how RefinedOps Publisher (“the app”), operated by Justin Eplin for The Refined G and Gaijin GT-R, handles information when it connects to TikTok and other social media platforms.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool. It is used only by its owner, Justin Eplin, to publish his own content to his own accounts (on TikTok: @therefinedg and @gaijingtr). It is not offered to other users, and it does not collect information from members of the public.

Information the app accesses

When the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app may access only the permissions he approves:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): the account’s open ID, display name and avatar. This is used only to confirm that the correct account is connected before anything is published.
  • Video upload and publishing (scopes video.upload and video.publish): used only to upload and publish videos that the account owner created and chose to post, along with their captions.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which let the app act on the owner’s behalf for the permissions above.

The app does not read direct messages, followers’ personal information, contacts, or location, and it does not access any account other than the owner’s own.

How information is used

Information is used only to publish the owner’s content and to confirm that a post succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information obtained through TikTok or any other platform with third parties. Information is sent only to the platform being published to (for example, TikTok), as required to make the post.

Storage and security

Tokens and publishing records are stored only on the owner’s private computer, in a secrets folder that is kept separate from the app’s code and is never published. They are not stored on any third-party server.

Retention

Access and refresh tokens are kept only while the connection is in use and are deleted when the connection is removed. Basic publishing records (such as the date a post was made and its post ID) are kept for up to 12 months for troubleshooting and then deleted.

Deletion and revoking access

The app’s access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted. To request deletion of any information the app holds, email dr0id1999@gmail.com; requests are completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
